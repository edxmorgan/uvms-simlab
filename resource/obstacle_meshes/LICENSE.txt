Modifications for UVMS-SimLab:
- Decode glTF normalized integer attributes before geometry import.
- Bake scene-node transforms and concatenate all triangle parts.
- Convert right-handed Y-up/Z-longitudinal coordinates to Z-up/X-longitudinal.
- Center the local bounding box on the obstacle origin.
- Export binary STL for collision and material-bearing Z-up COLLADA for display;
  retain the source's metre units and separate solid colour materials.
- Approximate PBR roughness with COLLADA shininess for the RViz renderer.

These are stylized rigid surface meshes, not scientifically validated animal
anatomy. No animation or rig is bundled; sources use colours, not image textures.
FCL uses the same
triangle surface as the displayed obstacle. Catalog scales are experiment-sized
presets (1 m fish; 2 m other animals), not claims of biological size.
